8 Hours
to Apex

Eight hours. Four fractured minds. One militarized corporate state.

08:00:00

Dean has eight hours before the code in her skull cooks her brain. Your clock started when you opened this page.

Forcibly injected with a decaying, mind-splintering cyber-weapon, a cynical data courier has exactly eight hours to breach a sovereign corporate state and infiltrate its mainframe before her brain liquefies.

The Iron Expanse is built in strips: slums, refineries, drowned tunnels, floating markets, a fortress bridge, and a white citadel at the end. Every district is sealed off from the next, and the people who run them want Dean stopped.

She has no cause and no side. She has a payload she was never meant to carry, a CEO who wants it back, and a map that only runs one way.

Six districts, one direction.

Dean enters each one with less time left than the last.

  1. 08:00

    The Marrow

    Subterranean trenches and slums. Where the clock starts.

  2. 07:00

    The Kiln

    Deep refineries that run on poisoned air and never shut down.

  3. 06:00

    The Concourse

    A drowned rail line under the bay. No signal, no witnesses, no easy way out.

  4. 05:00

    The Flood

    Floating night markets on the water of an old war crater.

  5. 04:00

    The Span

    A bridge built to work as a wall.

  6. 03:00

    The Apex

    The spotless white citadel, and the mainframe at its center.

She isn't alone in her head.

Dean is a 22-year-old data courier with three rules: don't look at the payload, don't ask who sent it, never pick a side. Then someone injected the payload into her.

The code didn't just infect her. It split into three minds, each rooted in a different part of her brain, and each one wants the wheel.

The three who share her skull.
